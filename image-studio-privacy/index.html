HuntGo Image Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Pycode Ltd

HuntGo Image Studio runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and it has no servers.

What the extension does with data

  • Product images and titles are read from the marketplace page you are on, only to build your listing image, and are processed locally on your device.
  • Background removal runs on your device (WebAssembly). Images are never uploaded.
  • Your design settings and your optional Google Gemini API key are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) and never leave your device — except that, if you choose to use the AI copy feature, your product title is sent to Google’s Gemini API using your own key, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Exported images are saved to your own computer via Chrome’s downloads.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
